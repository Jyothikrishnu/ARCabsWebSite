About AR Cabs

More than a cab service.

We connect reliable vehicle owners with customers and businesses that need dependable transportation.

AR Cabs is built around a simple idea: provide reliable transportation while creating a transparent and professional experience for vehicle owners.

Our owner-focused approach allows vehicle partners to keep track of their trips, kilometers, billing statements and payment confirmations through our dedicated owner application.

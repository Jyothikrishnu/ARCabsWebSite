1. Introduction

Welcome to AR Cabs Owner ("App"), operated by [Legal Business Name] ("AR Cabs", "we", "us", or "our").

AR Cabs Owner is an application intended for authorized cab owners associated with AR Cabs. The application allows authorized owners to access information related to their vehicles, trips, billing, earnings, payment status and related account information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we handle, how we use it, how it is stored and protected, and how you can contact us regarding privacy matters.

2. Account Creation and Registration

AR Cabs Owner does not provide a user registration or account-creation facility within the application.

Users cannot create an account through the App.

Owner accounts are created and managed by authorized AR Cabs administrators outside the application. The administrator may create an owner record and provide the owner with login credentials.

The App only provides authentication and access to an account that has already been provisioned by AR Cabs.

3. Information We Handle

3.1 Owner Information

  • Name
  • Mobile number
  • Authentication information
  • Account status

3.2 Vehicle Information

  • Vehicle registration number
  • Vehicle model
  • Vehicle-related billing information
  • Vehicle ownership information

3.3 Billing and Financial Information

The App may display information related to cab operations and billing, including:

  • Billing month
  • Fortnight information
  • Number of trips
  • Approved kilometers
  • Total kilometers
  • Rate per kilometer
  • Owner amount
  • Parking charges
  • GPS-related penalties
  • Other applicable charges
  • Final payable amount
  • Payment confirmation status

3.4 Payment Confirmation

When an owner confirms receipt of payment through the App, we may record the confirmation status, billing month, account identifier, date and time, and source of confirmation.

3.5 Technical Information

Depending on the version of the App and the services used, limited technical information may be processed to operate, secure and troubleshoot the application.

4. How We Use Information

Information is used for purposes related to operating and managing the AR Cabs service, including:

  • Authenticating authorized owners
  • Providing application access
  • Displaying owner and vehicle information
  • Displaying billing and earnings statements
  • Displaying trip and kilometer information
  • Recording payment confirmations
  • Maintaining business records
  • Managing owner access
  • Providing customer support
  • Maintaining application security
  • Resolving technical problems
  • Complying with applicable laws

We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising personalization.

5. Storage and Processing

AR Cabs currently uses Google Sheets and Google Apps Script as part of its backend and data-management infrastructure.

Information transmitted between the App and backend services is intended to use secure HTTPS connections.

Access to owner and billing information is restricted to authorized personnel and authorized application users.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be shared or made accessible when necessary for legitimate business or operational purposes, including authorized AR Cabs personnel, technology providers and where required by applicable law.

7. Payment Confirmation Emails

When an owner confirms receipt of payment, AR Cabs may send an email notification to relevant recipients, such as the owner and authorized AR Cabs administrators.

These emails may contain payment confirmation and related billing information and are used for business communication and record keeping.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled through the App and backend systems.

However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain billing and business records, maintain payment-confirmation records, resolve disputes, and meet applicable legal, accounting, regulatory or security requirements.

When information is no longer required for a legitimate purpose, AR Cabs may delete, anonymize or securely dispose of it.

10. Account Deactivation

Owner accounts are provisioned and managed by AR Cabs administrators.

An administrator may deactivate an owner's access when necessary. A deactivated account cannot be used to access the App.

Account deactivation does not necessarily mean that historical business, billing, accounting or legally required records are immediately deleted.

11. Children's Privacy

AR Cabs Owner is intended for authorized adult business users and is not directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App.

12. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party technology and infrastructure services necessary to operate the application and backend.

These services may include Google services used for backend data storage, processing, email delivery, hosting or other operational purposes.

Third-party services may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy
